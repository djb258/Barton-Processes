## The Brand Story

**INSURANCE** = svg.agency — the established industry side. 25 years. Authority.
**INFORMATICS** = weewee.me — the IT/data/systems side. 25 years. Precision.
**`//`** = the join. In code, `//` is a comment, a namespace separator, a path delimiter. It says "what follows is the truth." It encodes the Roman numeral II (Insurance Informatics) and the IT syntax simultaneously. The mark IS the merger of the two domains.

The brand doesn't explain itself. The name, the mark, and the wordmark together say: someone built a system where a market used to have only products.

**Rule:** The `//` is always in monospace (Courier New). Everything else is Helvetica Neue. The font contrast between mark and wordmark IS the story — code syntax meeting corporate precision.

**Weight contrast on wordmark:** INSURANCE bold = the sector (you know this). INFORMATICS light = the differentiator (you didn't have this). Never flip this.

## Logomark — The `//` Mark

**Concept:** The `//` (double-slash) simultaneously encodes:
1. **IT syntax** — comment delimiter in every major programming language; namespace/path separator. Signals the weewee.me side.
2. **Roman numeral II** — Insurance Informatics initials, visually. Two strokes.
3. **The join operator** — weewee.me `//` svg.agency. The mark is the bridge between the two 25-year domains.
4. **"What follows is the truth"** — in code, `//` precedes what matters. Insurance Informatics is the comment that industry never wrote.

**Files:**
- `logos/insurance-informatics-horizontal.svg` — primary lockup (navy mark box + wordmark, side by side)
- `logos/insurance-informatics-stacked.svg` — stacked lockup (`//` centered above wordmark)
- `logos/insurance-informatics-mark-only.svg` — icon only (navy sq, blue `//`, for favicons, social avatars)

**Mark rendering:**
- Always in Courier New or system monospace — never in Helvetica
- Always electric blue `#2563EB` — never navy, never white
- When on navy background: blue on navy (high readability)
- When on white background: use the navy mark box as container, or blue text directly

**Clear space:** Minimum 1× the mark height on all sides.

**Don't:**
- Render `//` in a sans-serif font
- Change the color of `//` to navy or white
- Add effects, gradients, or drop shadows
- Separate the `//` from the wordmark in any lockup — it precedes, it doesn't stand alone unless using mark-only version
