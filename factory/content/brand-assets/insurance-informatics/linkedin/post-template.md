## TEMPLATE A — "What Nobody Talks About" Format

```
Most benefits brokers don't know what you're paying for.

Not because they're dishonest.

Because they were never built to know.

[CORE INSIGHT — 2-3 sentences on a specific cost driver, claim trend, or compliance gap]

This is what Insurance Informatics fixes.

[SPECIFIC MECHANIC — what we actually do differently, one sentence]

If you want to see it applied to your plan, I'll model it in 20 minutes.

You either see the problem — or you don't.
```

**Best for:** Cold prospects, HR directors, CFOs
**CTA type:** Model offer (non-pressuring)

---

## Rules (Barton Doctrine)

1. Never start with "I" (LinkedIn algorithm + tone)
2. Short sentences. One idea per line.
3. No exclamation points.
4. No stock phrases: "game-changer," "passionate," "excited to announce," "delighted"
5. CTA is always a binary: act or accept that you didn't.
6. If you can't name the cost, don't make the claim.
7. The post introduces the one-pager. The one-pager introduces the conversation.
